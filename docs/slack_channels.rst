==============
Slack Channels
==============

The `Academy Software Foundation <https://www.aswf.io/>`__ (ASWF) hosts a Slack 
workspace with channels for discussing various USD topics.

`ASWF Slack workspace <http://slack.aswf.io/>`__

The #wg-usd channel covers all topics, but look for additional channels such as
#wg-usd-games, #wg-usd-camera, #wg-usd-assets, and more to join conversations
on your domain of interest.
